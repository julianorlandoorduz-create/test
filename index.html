Analyst notes — data quality & performance signals
Critical Won # and Won $ are 100% empty across all 173 events. Without closed-won tracking, ROI defense to finance is impossible and budget justification is purely directional.
Critical Pipeline (OPP $) recorded for only 19 of 83 completed events — 77% missing. The $12.1M figure almost certainly understates true pipeline generated.
Data gap AQL/MQL data covers only ~20 events. 81% of the portfolio has no lead data — either leads aren't being requested or registration isn't tracked systematically.
Data gap Industry vertical is blank or "All" for ~70% of events. Vertical penetration reporting — education, health, retail, hospitality — is not viable with current data quality.
Data gap GEO Approval Date is missing for 41% of completed events. Internal process compliance and retroactive approval detection cannot be audited without this field.
Performance Brazil drives the most pipeline ($7.8M), but this is heavily skewed by HPE Networking Day alone ($6.3M OPP). Excluding that event, Brazil and Mexico are roughly comparable.
Performance FY26 Q1 saw a sharp ramp-up to 48 completions from 13 in Q4. Q2 completions dropped to 22 while Q2 budget spend is highest at $1.14M — many events still in-flight.
Portfolio 36 declined + 13 cancelled = 28% of portfolio consumed planning resources without execution. Brazil accounts for 31 of the 36 declines — a significant drag on efficiency.
Positive Pipeline-to-investment ratio for events with data: ~5.8x ($12.1M on $2.1M spend). Even with incomplete OPP data, the directional signal for program ROI is strong.
ABX note 1:Few is the dominant approach (67 events), consistent with mid-funnel demand generation. However 12% of events have no ABX type assigned, limiting strategic segmentation accuracy.
